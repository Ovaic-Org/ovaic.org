Founded October 2026 · members welcome

Trust in AI should not rest on the word of whoever runs it.

OVAIC brings together organisations that make AI claims and decisions independently checkable: what evidence was used, who signed it, whether the sources are independent, and whether anyone can reproduce the result.

We build for future generations. Our aim is lasting public value that outlives any one company, not profit.

Open to everyone

We help those who help everyone. OVAIC is open to every person and every organisation, whatever their age, faith, nationality, origin or skin colour. A lack of money is never a reason to stay outside: an idea, the wish to contribute or a request for support is enough to apply. The Board considers every such request seriously and answers in writing.

We separate metaphysics and marketing from science that can be checked. Together we recognise pseudoscience, the wish for fame at any price and attempts to turn this common work into a private business. Money decides nothing here. What lasts is verification, trust and good work for the generations after us.

Memorandum, section 5

Why a consortium

AI systems now move money, shape public information and act on behalf of people. Many teams work on parts of the answer: signed evidence, provenance, oracle data, proofs of computation, transparency logs, agent permissions. No single company should decide how these fit together or which result counts. OVAIC is a neutral place to compare approaches on shared, public test cases and to publish what holds up.

Five principles

1

Independence

No member, chain, AI vendor or certification body controls the consortium or its results.

2

Reproducibility

A claim counts only if others can reproduce it from published code and data.

3

Open correction

Errors are corrected in public, with credit to whoever found them. A finding against a member's own method is welcome.

4

Plurality

Members compare different approaches. OVAIC does not crown one technology.

5

No verdicts on trust

OVAIC publishes methods and test results. It does not certify that any system or person can be trusted.

What we do

Open test bed

Shared, public test cases (attacks, edge cases, real data) against which any member method can be run and compared.

Interoperability

Profiles for how signed evidence, provenance records, oracle data, proofs of computation and transparency receipts fit together.

Joint reports

Reproducible results, published with code and data.

Open standards

Members contribute to the IETF, W3C, C2PA and other bodies in their own name.

Who can join

RoleWhoRights

Joining takes a short application and acceptance of the charter. The annual fee is graded by size, from CHF 100 for individuals to CHF 5,000 for companies with up to 999 staff; larger companies join as strategic members by agreement, in money or in kind (see Fees). Every member declares its products and does not vote on results that concern its own method.

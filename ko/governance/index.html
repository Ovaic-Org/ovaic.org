Trust & governance

OVAIC exists to make AI claims and decisions independently checkable. Its own governance follows the same rule: who is a member, who decides and who is connected to whom is public.

How decisions are made

  • Every member has one vote. Voting rights never depend on the fee.
  • A member does not vote on results that concern its own method or product.
  • The General Assembly elects the Board. Between assemblies the members may decide by a written resolution of all members (Art. 66 of the Swiss Civil Code).
  • By the first ordinary General Assembly, a majority of the Board comes from organisations independent of the initiator (Art. 26 of the statutes).

Money

  • Fees are graded by size and published.
  • Funds are used only for the purpose of the association.
  • The annual accounts are published with income by category and expenses by item.
  • Contributions above CHF 10,000 are published with the name of the giver, with the giver's consent. Contributions give no influence on methods or results.

회비

Openness

  • Everything OVAIC publishes is meant to be open: code under Apache 2.0, texts and test cases under CC BY 4.0. The IP policy was adopted with the other policies on 19 October 2026.
  • The statutes, charter, policies, memorandum and fees are public in six languages; the English version is binding.

Status, 28 October 2026: the association is not yet entered in the commercial register. The signed confirmations of the founding members are being collected for the archive.
